Privacy

Last changed 23 November 2026

In short:

  • PunchPace works without an account. Then your routines, settings and workouts stay on your device.
  • With an account, your routines and workouts are also stored with Google (Firebase), so they sync between your devices. Sound and other settings stay on each device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can export everything, and delete your account and all synced data yourself, in Settings.

Who is responsible

PunchPace is made by Age Poortinga in the Netherlands, who decides what happens with your data. Questions or requests: hello@punchpace.app.

Without an account

Your routines, settings and workout history are saved in your browser on this device. So is a random device code and the kind of device (like “Android phone”), used to show where a workout is running. None of this is sent anywhere. Opening Settings loads the code for signing in and syncing from Google’s servers, so Google sees your IP address, as any website you visit does. The security check below only starts once you sign in.

After you’ve signed in on a device, it also remembers which account it last synced with (its email address), so it never mixes in a different account by mistake. That stays on the device until you choose Sign out and clear or delete your account.

This storage is only what the app needs to work. There are no tracking cookies, so the app doesn’t ask for consent.

With an account

When you sign in with Google or GitHub, these are stored:

  • Your sign-in, in Firebase Authentication: your email address, name and profile photo link as Google or GitHub give them, which sign-in you used, and when you signed up and last signed in. Google may store this in the US.
  • Your routines and workouts, in the Firebase Realtime Database in Belgium: routines, their order, workout history, your parts of the day, and while a workout runs, its progress with the device code and kind of device, so your other devices can show it.
  • A security check: once you’re signed in, Google’s reCAPTCHA (Firebase App Check) checks that requests come from the real PunchPace app and not from someone misusing it. It looks at signals from your device and browser.

Your sign-in and your routines and workouts are used to give you the account and sync you asked for (the legal basis: carrying out what you signed up for). The security check and the server logs below keep the service safe from misuse (the legal basis: a legitimate interest in keeping it safe; you can object, see Your rights). Your email address isn’t used to send you anything; it only shows which account you’re signed in with.

Services involved

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, App Check with reCAPTCHA, and hosting of punchpace.app): Firebase privacy, Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub, if you sign in with GitHub, and for the app’s old address (agepoor.github.io/repflow): GitHub privacy statement.

Google and GitHub may keep the usual server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect their services. PunchPace doesn’t receive or use those. Both are US companies; data that goes to the US is protected under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and their standard data protection terms.

How long it’s kept

Until you delete it. Delete account in Settings removes your synced routines, workouts and sign-in right away, on every device. When you delete a single routine or workout, a small marker (its code and the time) stays in your account so your other devices remove it too; it goes when you delete the account. On a device, Sign out and clear removes your routines, history and account from it. To remove everything, settings included, uninstall the app or clear this site’s data in your browser.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Export JSON in Settings saves your routines, settings and workouts in a file. Settings shows the account you’re signed in with; email us for a copy of everything stored about your sign-in.
  • Correct it: edit your routines and history in the app.
  • Delete it: Delete account in Settings, or on a device, Sign out and clear.
  • Ask or object: email hello@punchpace.app; you’ll get an answer within a month.
  • Complain: to the Dutch data protection authority, Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Age

An account is for people aged 16 or over. Younger people need permission from a parent or guardian.
